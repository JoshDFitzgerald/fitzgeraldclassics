Some cars are special before you ever turn a wrench. This 1966 Corvette Convertible is a three-owner car, 100% numbers matching, and a true rare find. It had already been fully renovated more than 30 years ago, including a complete engine rebuild. The first photos show it the day I purchased it, and most people would have said it was fine just as it was. I could see why.

But I decided to give it a full renovation of my own. The photographs follow that decision through the work: opening up the interior, preparing the body, attending to the mechanical details, and bringing the silver finish back to life. With a car like this, the aim is to give it the care it deserves while preserving what makes it special.

One change I especially wanted was a better convertible top. The old vinyl top made way for a high-end black canvas top, giving the Corvette the true roadster look I had in mind. There is a photo of that new top in the gallery, along with the many less glamorous steps that make a renovation worthwhile.

The final photos show the car back home from the paint and body shop. Seeing that fresh silver paint in the garage was a milestone, but the story is still being written. I am now working on the last two projects: Vintage Air and an all-new interior. There is more to finish before I call this renovation complete, and I am looking forward to enjoying the result on the road.

Preserving Americana… one classic car at a time.
