A complete visual journey from a tired blue survivor to a refined white-and-turquoise American classic. This Fitzgerald Classics restoration follows the Galaxie through body preparation, paint, interior reconstruction, detail work and final assembly—preserving the character of Ford’s full-size 1960s convertible while giving every surface the attention it deserved.
