Purchased as a remarkably solid but unfinished 1957 Nash Metropolitan, this project is being completed with a simple philosophy: preserve the character of the car, correct what matters, and invest where the finished result will have the greatest impact. This living project follows the restoration from its as-acquired condition and mechanical foundation through body preparation, interior work, electrical sorting, paint, trim, final assembly and road testing.



Paint milestone — October 2026: Following the four coats of white applied on October 10, the white sections were carefully masked and the prepared upper body and hood received their yellow color. The latest photo sequence follows the car from primer and final masking through freshly applied yellow paint to the reveal of its yellow-and-white finish. Reflections across the panels capture the gloss, while the two-tone layout brings the Metropolitan's distinctive curves back into focus.



Current status: The yellow-and-white paint is now applied, marking a major step toward the finished car. The restoration continues with paint finishing as needed, glass, lights, trim and hood-ornament installation, upholstery, final assembly and road testing. The hood ornament has already been test-fitted for alignment, and the original bench-seat frame has been sandblasted, powder coated in satin black and reassembled, ready for upholstery.



Updated October 12, 2026. Preserving Americana, one car at a time.
