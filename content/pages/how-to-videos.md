Fitzgerald Classics curates practical, real-world how-to videos for classic car owners, enthusiasts and restorers who want to do the job right. Our goal is to bring together clear, useful guidance from experienced builders, restorers and creators across the classic car community.

The library covers everything from restoration techniques and mechanical repairs to wiring, EFI, air conditioning, brakes, suspension, driveline work, engine projects, maintenance and upgrades. Whether the video comes from our own work at Fitzgerald Classics or from another trusted source, we select content based on its usefulness, clarity and real-world value.

We look for videos that respect the character of classic vehicles while emphasizing good workmanship, reliability and practical solutions. Whether you’re planning a full restoration, tackling a weekend repair or trying to solve a stubborn problem, these how-to videos are here to help you move your project forward with confidence.
