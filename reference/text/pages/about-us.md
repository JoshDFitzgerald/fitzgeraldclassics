####### OUR STORY

## Restoring the Soul of America

At Fitzgerald Classics, we believe every vintage automobile tells a story worth preserving. Our mission is to honor the craftsmanship of the past while educating the enthusiasts of the future. Through meticulous restoration and a commitment to authenticity, we ensure that these rolling pieces of history continue to inspire for generations to come.

### Heritage & Restoration Philosophy

Every classic automobile is more than a machine—it is a living piece of Americana. Fitzgerald Classics documents the research, craftsmanship, challenges, and decisions involved in preserving these vehicles and the stories they carry.

Our philosophy is simple: respect the vehicle, understand its history, document the journey, and share what we learn. Whether restoring a car completely or carefully improving a survivor, the goal is to preserve its character for the next generation.
