Reflections across the panels capture the gloss, while the two-tone layout brings the Metropolitan's distinctive curves back into focus. / Current status: The yellow-and-white paint is now applied, marking a major step toward the finished car. The restoration continues with paint finishing as needed, glass, lights, trim and hood-ornament installation, upholstery, final assembly and road testing. The hood ornament has already been test-fitted for alignment, and the original bench-seat frame has been sandblasted, powder coated in satin black and reassembled, ready for upholstery. / Updated October 12, 2026. Preserving Americana, one car at a time.](/portfolio-collections/portfolio/1957-nash-metropolitan-restoration-in-progress)
